# IDEM Section 401 Water Quality Certification (Indiana)

**Status:** Process overview for coordination. **Not** a certification application.

---

## 1. What Section 401 is

Under **Clean Water Act §401**, a federal agency **may not** issue a license or permit for an activity that may result in a **discharge** into navigable waters unless the state (or authorized tribe) grants, conditions, waives, or denies a **Water Quality Certification**.

In Indiana, **IDEM** is the certifying authority. Certification is judged against Indiana water quality standards (**327 IAC 2**) and related state law.

**If USACE requires a §404 permit (including use of an NWP), you generally need IDEM 401** (unless a specific waiver or blanket pathway fully covers the activity under published conditions).

## 3. Pathways in Indiana

### A. General / NWP-linked certification

When USACE reissues Nationwide Permits, IDEM issues a **401 decision** that may:

- **Certify** listed NWPs **with conditions**
- **Deny** certain NWPs (forcing **individual** 401)
- Reflect Indiana-specific suspensions / RGP substitutions

If your activity fits an NWP that IDEM certified **and** you meet **every** federal NWP condition **and** every IDEM condition, you may proceed under that general certification pathway (sometimes via notification rather than a full individual WQC). If you exceed limits or miss conditions → **individual 401**.

### B. Regional General Permit (RGP) notification

For **minimal** impacts that fit IDEM’s RGP framework:

- Submit **RGP Notification** (historically ~30-day review; automatic approval unless IDEM objects)
- Used when impacts are small and mitigation is not required under IDEM rules

### C. Individual Section 401 WQC

Required when:

- Impacts are larger or to sensitive resources
- Mitigation is required
- Activity is outside general/NWP/RGP conditions
- IDEM denied 401 for that NWP category

Individual applications use IDEM forms for discharge of dredged/fill material to waters of the state / isolated wetlands as applicable.

### D. Pre-filing meeting

IDEM emphasizes a **Pre-filing Meeting Request** submitted a **minimum of 30 days** before the formal 401 application (per current IDEM 401 process pages). Build this into the schedule.

Online portal referenced by IDEM: [https://stormwater.idem.in.gov/](https://stormwater.idem.in.gov/) (confirm current application entry points on in.gov).

---

## 4. What IDEM looks for (substance)

- Accurate impact quantities (streams linear feet, wetland acreage)
- Avoidance and minimization
- Compensatory **mitigation** when required to meet WQS / no-net-loss policies
- Compliance with **327 IAC 2** designated uses and criteria
- Special waters (e.g., outstanding state resource waters, salmonid listings) — extra scrutiny
- Consistency with the federal permit description (401 and 404 must describe the **same** project)
