## Abstract / purpose

The supplied record describes a feature layer containing proposed, planned, and completed mapping footprints pertinent to the U.S. Mapping Coordination site. Footprints are supplied by multiple federal, state, local, private, and academic stakeholders to inform coordinated mapping activity. The layer provides attribution for filtering, sharing, and cross-referencing.
