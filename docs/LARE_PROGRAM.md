# Indiana LARE — Lake and River Enhancement

**Administrator:** IDNR Division of Fish & Wildlife  
**Official home:** [Lake & River Enhancement](https://www.in.gov/dnr/fish-and-wildlife/wildlife-resources/lake-and-river-enhancement/)  
**Program manual:** [LARE Program Manual](https://www.in.gov/dnr/fish-and-wildlife/wildlife-resources/lake-and-river-enhancement/lare-program-manual)  
**Reports:** [larereports.dnr.in.gov](https://larereports.dnr.in.gov/)  
**Contact:** lare@dnr.IN.gov

LARE is a **competitive cost-share** program funded from the **boat registration / LARE fee**. It is **not** a LOMA, No-Rise, or HMA substitute.

## Goals

- Reduce non-point sediment and nutrient pollution to meet or surpass state water-quality standards
- Protect/enhance aquatic habitat for fish and wildlife
- Keep **publicly accessible** lakes and streams viable for recreation (boating, fishing, etc.)

## Eligible funding purposes (DNR list)

1. Investigations of problems affecting a lake or stream segment  
2. Evaluation and recommended actions  
3. Cost-share with upstream land users for sediment/nutrient-reducing practices  
4. Match for qualifying federal funds  
5. Feasibility studies for remediation  
6. Engineering design and construction of remedial measures  
7. Water-quality monitoring of public lakes  
8. Management of invasive aquatic vegetation  
9. Sediment removal from qualifying lakes  
10. Logjam removal from qualifying rivers  

## Application cycle

- **Deadline:** applications due **January 15** of the year grants are awarded

## Cost share (practice notes)

- Local sponsors generally share **at least ~20%** of project cost (recent award announcements).  
- Watershed land treatment / practice cost-share is often described as **~80% LARE** on eligible practices with per-practice caps (e.g. historical ~$35k NTE examples for some logjam-type awards — **confirm current policy PDFs** before budgeting).  
- SWCDs may receive a small administrative percentage when sponsoring WLTP grants.  

Always use the **current** manual PDFs, not secondary summaries.

## Scope-of-services templates (manual)

DNR publishes Word/PDF scopes for sediment removal plans, AVM, design/build, engineering design, feasibility, lake diagnostic, watershed diagnostic, water-quality monitoring, post-construction monitoring, and construction.

## Fit with Bonebank / PTDT

| Path | Role |
|------|------|
| LOMA | FEMA map amendment |
| IDNR floodway / No-Rise | IC 14-28-1 / 312 IAC 10 construction |
| HMA | FEMA mitigation grants via state |
| **LARE** | Public lake/stream sediment, logjam, invasives, watershed BMPs via local sponsor |

A private parcel digital twin does **not** automatically qualify. A **local sponsor** (SWCD, lake association, watershed group, local government) would need a **qualifying public-water** project and a January 15 application.
