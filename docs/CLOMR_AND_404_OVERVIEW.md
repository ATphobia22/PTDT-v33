# CLOMR process & USACE Section 404 — overview

See full Bonebank checklist: `certification/09_CIF_CLOMR_404_BONEBANK_CHECKLIST.md`.

## CLOMR in one paragraph

FEMA **CLOMR** = conditional, **pre-build** acceptance that proposed works *would* support a future flood map revision. Requires PE hydraulics (MT-2), community coordination, and usually **LOMR** after as-builts. **No-Rise (0.00')** is the alternative when the project truly does not increase BFEs in a FEMA floodway. Neither replaces **IDNR Construction in a Floodway** under IC 14-28-1.

## Section 404 in one paragraph

**USACE Section 404** (Clean Water Act) covers **fill/dredge in WOTUS**. Ohio River / wetland / channel work at Bonebank-class sites may need Louisville District authorization (NWP with PCN or individual permit), often with **IDEM 401**. Separate from IDNR CIF and from FEMA CLOMR/No-Rise.
