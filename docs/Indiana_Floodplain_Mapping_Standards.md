# Indiana Floodplain Mapping Standards

## Dual-Layer System
Indiana uses two complementary map layers:

| Layer | Source | Primary Use |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **National Flood Hazard Layer (NFHL / FIRM)** | FEMA | Flood insurance, mandatory purchase, official SFHA |
| **Best Available Floodplain Layer (BAFL)** | IDNR Division of Water | Local permitting, Construction in a Floodway, planning; adds >18,000 stream miles of Zone A-quality studies with BFEs and floodways |

## Indiana Floodplain Information Portal (INFIP)
- Interactive viewer of FEMA + BAFL data
- Generates **Floodplain Analysis and Regulatory Assessment (FARA)**
- FARA is required for:
  - Local permits in Zone A
  - Unmapped areas or known flood-prone sites
  - Upstream drainage area > 1 square mile
  - FEMA LOMA applications in Zone A
- Users must download and keep their own FARA copy

**Portal:** https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/8bf315c8212f44c387f528be429327c5/

## Governing Rules
- **IC 14-28-1** – Flood Control Act
- **312 IAC 10** – Floodplain Management (delineation, freeboard, floodway rules)
- Vertical datum: **NAVD88**
- Many communities use Flood Protection Grade = BFE + 2 ft

## Practical Rules for 13101 Bonebank Road
1. Always check both effective FIRM and BAFL via INFIP.
2. Generate and archive a current FARA for the site coordinates.
3. Use FARA / FIS BFE when preparing LOMA elevation data.
4. Work in a DNR-delineated floodway still requires Construction in a Floodway approval even if outside the FEMA SFHA.
