# IDEM Clean Water Act Section 319(h) and 205(j)

**Agency:** Indiana Department of Environmental Management — Nonpoint Source (Office of Water Quality)  
**319 page:** [Clean Water Act Section 319(h) Grants](https://www.in.gov/idem/nps/funding/clean-water-act-section-319h-grants)  
**Application instructions:** [319/205(j) Grant Application Instructions](https://www.in.gov/idem/nps/progress-evaluation/319205j-grant-application-instructions/)  
**Funding hub:** [IDEM NPS Funding](https://www.in.gov/idem/nps/funding/)

These are **federal pass-through** nonpoint-source / planning grants. They are **not** floodway permits, LOMA, LARE, or CWI.

## Section 319(h)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Reduce nonpoint source pollution: assessments, TMDLs, watershed management plans (EPA **nine elements**), technical assistance, education, BMP implementation |
| **Eligible** | Nonprofits, universities, local/state/federal agencies, municipalities, watershed development commissions |
| **Match** | Generally **40%** non-federal (cash or in-kind). **New watershed management plan only:** 319 may cover **80%** with **20%** match. Federal funds cannot be used as match. |
| **Duration** | Often 2–3 years |
| **Cycle** | One competitive round per year (see annual solicitation PDF) |
| **Scale** | On the order of ~$2M statewide annually (varies) |

### Application materials (official)

- Instructions PDF (Nonpoint Source Management Program Section 319 Grant Application)  
- State Form **49367** (IDEM Agency Forms)  
- Budget worksheet (XLS)  
- Solicitation announcement (e.g. FFY 2027 solicitation listed electronic deadline **Sep 1, 2026** 11:59 pm EST — confirm current year PDF)  
- Priority watershed map/list (scoring favors priority areas)

IDEM does **not** accept applications outside the solicitation window.

### Build America, Buy America (BABA)

319 projects with covered infrastructure may trigger BABA. Guidance notes possible exceptions (e.g. agreement under $250k, no permanently incorporated covered products, agricultural/conservation BMPs only, private non-public-use projects). Confirm with IDEM/EPA for the specific scope.

### Cost-share program note

319 funds generally **cannot** pay for compliance with permits, but can fund water-quality BMPs that provide benefits beyond pure compliance (see IDEM 319 cost-share requirements pages).

## Section 205(j)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Water quality **management planning** (nature/extent/causes of pollution; plans to resolve) |
| **Eligible** | Municipal/county governments, regional planning commissions, other **public** organizations |
| **Not eligible** | For-profits, nonprofits, private associations, individuals |
| **Match** | **None** required |
| **Restrictions** | Cannot fund dredging, drainage/flood control, permit fees, implementation projects, NPDES compliance/enforcement |
| **Amount** | Varies; historically on the order of ~$350k annually statewide |

IDEM often selects planning proposals through the **319 solicitation** process for 205(j)/604(b) funding — contact a Watershed Specialist before assuming a separate track.

## Process timing

From NOI through contract start, funding can become available **more than a year** after IDEM approves the application. Plan multi-year calendars accordingly.

## Fit with Bonebank / PTDT

| Need | 319 / 205(j)? |
|------|----------------|
| LOMA elevations | No |
| IDNR No-Rise / floodway construction | No |
| Watershed BMPs / WMP / education in a priority watershed | Possible for an **eligible organization** |
| Private homeowner as direct grantee | Generally **no** |

Use a watershed group, SWCD partner, or local government as applicant if pursuing 319.
