# USACE Nationwide Permit 27 — Aquatic Habitat Restoration, Enhancement & Establishment

**Status:** Reference for PE/consultant scoping. **Not** an authorization.  
Confirm the **current** NWP reissuance text, Louisville District regional conditions, and IDEM 401 conditions before relying on NWP 27.

---

## 1. What NWP 27 is for

NWP 27 authorizes activities in **waters of the United States** whose **primary purpose** is the **restoration, enhancement, or establishment** of aquatic habitat (including wetlands and streams), such that the project results in a **net increase** in aquatic resource functions and services.

Typical examples (when they truly fit):

- Wetland restoration / re-establishment
- Stream restoration and aquatic habitat enhancement
- Removal of small dams or fish barriers (within NWP limits)
- Certain compensatory mitigation projects (banks / in-lieu fee), subject to NWP terms

**Not** a general “flood berm construction” permit. Earthen flood-control berms, tide gates, and conveyance armor are usually evaluated under other NWPs, **Indiana RGP**, or an **individual 404** — unless a discrete project component is genuinely habitat restoration with a net aquatic benefit.

---

## 2. Core requirements (conceptual — verify current Federal Register text)

| Requirement | Practical meaning |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Purpose** | Aquatic ecosystem restoration / enhancement / establishment must be the **primary** purpose |
| **Net benefit** | Activities should produce a **net increase** in aquatic functions; pure flood-control fill does not qualify by relabeling |
| **Minimal effects** | Must meet all NWP **General Conditions** (navigation, endangered species, historic properties, water quality, etc.) |
| **PCN** | **Pre-Construction Notification** to the District when required by NWP terms, general conditions (e.g. ESA, historic properties), or **regional conditions** |
| **Mitigation** | Often **not** required for pure restoration that creates net aquatic gains; if wetland **losses** exceed thresholds and PCN is required, compensatory mitigation rules can still apply — follow current NWP 27 notes |
| **Reporting** | Some restoration activities require documentation / reporting in lieu of full delineation packages (see current Note language) |
| **401 WQC** | Federal 404/NWP cannot be used without **IDEM Section 401** certification (general or individual) for discharges in Indiana |

### Pre-Construction Notification (PCN) — when to expect it

Submit a PCN to **USACE Louisville District** when any of the following apply (non-exhaustive):

- NWP 27 text or district **regional conditions** require PCN for the activity type
- **General Condition** triggers (e.g., possible effects to listed species, historic properties, wild/scenic rivers)
- Activity used as **compensatory mitigation** (monitoring often required)
- Uncertainty — when in doubt, coordinate early with the District

District has a completeness clock on PCNs (commonly ~30 days to declare complete/incomplete). Do not start work until authorized under the NWP + 401 pathway.

## 3. Indiana / Louisville District realities

- **Louisville District** regulates much of southern Indiana including the Ohio River corridor (Posey County).
- Indiana has used **Regional General Permit (RGP)** mechanisms and has **suspended** certain NWPs in the past for activities covered by RGP #1 (e.g., some bank stabilization / minor discharges historically). **Always check which NWPs are active vs suspended in Indiana for the current cycle.**
- **NWP 27** has generally remained available subject to **IDEM’s 401 conditions** on the NWP package — still verify the **active** IDEM certification decision for the current NWP suite.
- Flood-defense components (Archimedes berms, gates, riprap outfalls) should be scoped **separately**: habitat basins might support an NWP 27 discussion; structural floodworks usually will not.

## 4. Bonebank / Archimedes Line — honest fit test

| Component | Likely NWP 27? |
|-----------|----------------|
| Engineered **flood berms** (primary purpose: backwater control) | **No** — flood control, not aquatic restoration |
| **Retention basins** designed mainly for flood storage | **Unlikely** unless redesigned/documented as wetland/habitat restoration with net aquatic gain |
| Ecological buffer / wetland re-establishment tied to cut for compensatory storage | **Maybe** for that **component only**, if PE/ecologist documents restoration purpose and net benefit |
| Outfall **riprap** / channel armor | Usually **other** 404 path (NWP or individual), not NWP 27 |
| Tide / drainage gates | Not NWP 27 |

**Do not force NWP 27** onto a flood-control project to avoid individual permitting. Misuse risks enforcement and permit invalidation.

## 5. Checklist if pursuing NWP 27 for a restoration *component*

- [ ] Written purpose statement: aquatic restoration primary
- [ ] Baseline vs proposed aquatic functions (metrics)
- [ ] Wetland/stream delineation or NWP-allowed substitute documentation
- [ ] PCN package to Louisville District if required
- [ ] IDEM 401 path (general certification conditions vs individual)
- [ ] ESA / NHPA / tribal coordination as required by general conditions
- [ ] Monitoring plan if mitigation or permit special conditions require it
- [ ] Separate IDNR CIF / local floodplain / FEMA No-Rise or CLOMR as applicable
