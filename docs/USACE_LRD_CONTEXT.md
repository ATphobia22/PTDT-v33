# USACE context — Great Lakes and Ohio River Division

**Agency:** U.S. Army Corps of Engineers  
**National:** [usace.army.mil](https://www.usace.army.mil/)  
**Division (Indiana region):** [Great Lakes and Ohio River Division (LRD)](https://www.lrd.usace.army.mil/)

## Mission areas (high level)

| Area | Role |
|------|------|
| **Civil Works** | Navigation channels, dams/reservoirs, flood risk management, dredging, hydropower where applicable |
| **Military Construction** | Facilities for Army/Air Force |
| **Regulatory** | Clean Water Act / Rivers and Harbors Act permits (wetlands, waters of the U.S.) |
| **Emergency response** | Support under the National Response Framework |
| **Recreation** | Reservoir recreation areas |

Workforce is predominantly civilian; structure is divisions and districts under LRD for the Ohio River / Great Lakes geography.

## Relevance to Point Township / Wabash–Ohio confluence

- **Hydraulic models of record** for regulatory No-Rise packages should use **HEC-RAS** (USACE-maintained software) under PE supervision — not a custom repo “coupler” as model-of-record.  
- **Section 404 / Section 10** permits may apply if work affects waters/wetlands — separate from IDNR floodway permits and FEMA LOMA.  
- LRD / district offices handle regional civil works and regulatory contacts; verify the correct **district** for Posey County before any permit pre-app meeting.  
- Basin planning documents (e.g. Ohio River Basin PAS studies) are **context**, not site approval.

## What USACE is not for this private parcel workflow

- USACE does not issue FEMA LOMA letters (that is FEMA).  
- USACE does not replace Indiana DNR Construction in a Floodway / No-Rise.  
- Software SHA hashes are not USACE certifications.

## Practical next contacts

2. Use USACE HEC-RAS for PE floodway models.  
3. If wetland impacts are possible, request a **jurisdictional determination / pre-application** path through the district regulatory branch.
