# Clean Water Indiana (CWI)

**Authority context:** IC 14-32-8 (program framework)  
**Administered under:** Indiana State Department of Agriculture (ISDA) — Division of Soil Conservation, directed by the **State Soil Conservation Board (SSCB)**  
**Official page:** [Clean Water Indiana — ISDA](https://www.in.gov/isda/divisions/soil-conservation/clean-water-indiana/)

CWI provides financial assistance for **sediment and nutrient reduction** and related conservation work, including support tied to Soil and Water Conservation Districts (SWCDs). It is separate from LARE (DNR Fish & Wildlife) and from IDEM Section 319.

## What it funds (high level)

- Competitive grants for targeted sediment/nutrient reduction projects (multi-year proposals possible)
- Local matching / SWCD-related funding streams under SSCB direction
- Education, technical assistance, training, and cost-share style conservation practices (see current guidance)

## Application (current practice)

- Submit through the **Indiana Grants Management System (GMS)** / Indiana Grants Management Portal  
- Request GMS access per ISDA instructions on the CWI page  
- Follow the **year-specific** CWI Grant Guidance PDF and Application Template (e.g. 2026 / 2027 documents linked from ISDA)  
- Guidance typically requires addressing **State priority resource concerns** (document at least two, per recent guidance language)  
- Attachment limits apply (recent guidance: ≤3 attachments, ≤6 pages total, with letters of commitment excluded from the page cap)  
- Letters of commitment from boards/partners outlining financial or in-kind roles are expected  

Confirm deadlines and scoring on the live ISDA page and the year’s scoring rubric PDF.

## Relationship to other Indiana water funding

| Program | Agency | Focus |
|---------|--------|--------|
| **CWI** | ISDA / SSCB | Sediment/nutrient, SWCD-linked conservation |
| **LARE** | DNR Fish & Wildlife | Public lakes/streams: sediment, logjams, invasives, diagnostics |
| **IDEM 319 / 205(j)** | IDEM | Federal nonpoint / watershed planning grants |
| **HMA (BRIC/HMGP/FMA)** | FEMA via IDHS | Hazard mitigation infrastructure |

IDEM’s nonpoint funding page lists LARE and CWI as **non-IDEM** state sources for watershed coordinators:  
https://www.in.gov/idem/nps/funding/non-idem-funding/lare-cwi-and-additional-information/

## Project note

CWI is a **sponsor/SWCD-mediated** conservation path. It does not issue floodway No-Rise certifications or FEMA LOMA determinations.
