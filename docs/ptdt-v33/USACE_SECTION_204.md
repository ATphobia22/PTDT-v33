# USACE Section 204 — Beneficial Use of Dredged Material

**Authority:** WRDA 1992 §204, as amended (33 U.S.C. §2326), Continuing Authorities Program (CAP).

## What it is

USACE may implement projects for **aquatic/ecosystem habitat** protection-restoration-creation **or** storm-damage reduction **in connection with dredging** for construction or O&M of an **existing authorized Federal navigation project**.

## Eligibility gates (fail closed if unmet)

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Navigation nexus | Tied to authorized federal navigation dredging |
| Non-federal sponsor | Public agency (typical); some nonprofits; private only if no O&M required |
| Letter of Intent | Starts Federal Interest Determination |
| Not mitigation substitute | **Cannot** be used solely to meet unrelated mitigation/remediation mandates |
| Appropriations | Subject to CAP funding availability |

## Cost share (district sheets vary slightly — confirm Louisville / local district)

| Phase | Typical share |
|---|---|
| Feasibility / study | **100% Federal** (common CAP pattern) |
| Design + implementation | **65% Federal / 35% non-Federal** on costs **above the Base Plan** (least-cost disposal without beneficial use) |
| O&M after completion | **100% non-Federal** |
| Federal project limit | Often cited **$10M–$15M** per activity (use current district CAP fact sheet) |

Base Plan = disposal that would occur **without** the beneficial-use increment. Only the **incremental** cost is cost-shared.

## PTDT implication for Archimedes Line fill

- Clean dredged clay is **not** automatically free material.  
- Requires: sponsor LOI → FID → feasibility → PPA → navigation schedule alignment.  
- PDF claims of **$22M in-kind** remain **unbooked** until USACE documents valuation.  
- Grant-stack matching may **reference** future in-kind **only with agency letters**.

## Process sketch

1. Non-federal sponsor LOI to district  
2. Federal Interest Determination (fed expense when funded)  
3. Feasibility (alternatives, NEPA, cost)  
4. Project Partnership Agreement  
5. Design / construction with LERRDs by sponsor  
6. Sponsor O&M
