## A. CLOMR process (FEMA)

### What it is

A **Conditional Letter of Map Revision (CLOMR)** is FEMA’s **pre-construction** comment that a **proposed** project *would* meet NFIP criteria for a future map change **if** built as modeled. It is **not** a construction permit and **not** a final map change.

After construction and as-builts, you typically pursue a **Letter of Map Revision (LOMR)** to update the effective FIRM/FIS.

### When you need it (vs No-Rise)

| Situation in **FEMA-mapped floodway** | Path |
|---------------------------------------|------|
| Hydraulics show **0.00 ft** rise (all relevant profiles) | PE **No-Rise Certification** to community + still need **IDNR CIF** if state floodway |
| Hydraulics show **any** rise > 0.00 ft | **CLOMR** before local floodway/floodplain permit; **LOMR** after build |

**Indiana note:** IDNR may still allow up to **0.14 ft** cumulative under the Flood Control Act, but **FEMA/local NFIP** for FEMA floodways require **0.00 ft** or CLOMR/LOMR. A DNR CIF alone does **not** replace No-Rise.

### High-level CLOMR steps

1. **Confirm zone** — Effective FIRM floodway vs fringe; pull FIS profiles for the reach.
2. **Hire Indiana PE** — Hydraulic model (often HEC-RAS 1D/2D) per FEMA standards.
3. **Build model stack** (typical):
   - Duplicative effective
   - Corrected effective (if needed)
   - Existing conditions
   - Proposed conditions
4. **Prepare MT-2 package** — Forms, narratives, digital models, annotated FIRM, property owner notifications as required.
5. **Submit** via FEMA Online LOMC / MT-2 process (community acknowledgment often required).
6. **Respond to FEMA comments** until CLOMR issued (or denied).
7. **Build only after** required local/state permits that rely on the CLOMR are in hand.
8. **As-built survey + LOMR** — Prove constructed works match CLOMR assumptions.

### CLOMR vs LOMA (do not confuse)

| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **LOMA** | Structure/parcel is **naturally** above BFE (often no fill) |
| **CLOMR/LOMR** | **Project changes** flood hazard mapping (fill, berms, channel work, etc.) |

Pure natural-ground LOMA for the house is a **separate** path from floodway **construction** permits.

## B. USACE Section 404 (and related)

### What Section 404 is

Under the **Clean Water Act**, **Section 404** regulates discharge of **dredged or fill material** into **waters of the United States** (WOTUS), including many wetlands and stream channels. The **U.S. Army Corps of Engineers** issues 404 permits; **EPA** retains oversight.

**Louisville District** covers much of southern Indiana including the Ohio River corridor — coordinate with Louisville Regulatory for Posey County sites.

### Related authorities often bundled in review

| Authority | Agency | Typical issue at Bonebank-class sites |
|-----------|--------|--------------------------------------|
| CWA 404 (fill/dredge) | USACE | Berm toes, basin excavation spoil, outfall armor in WOTUS |
| Rivers & Harbors Act **Section 10** | USACE | Work in **navigable** waters (Ohio River) |
| CWA 401 water quality | **IDEM** (Indiana) | State certification tied to 404 |
| Indiana floodway CIF | **IDNR** | Separate state floodway permit |
| Local floodplain | **Posey County** | Local development permit + NFIP |

### Individual vs Nationwide / Regional

- **Nationwide Permits (NWPs)** — Pre-authorized categories with limits (e.g., certain bank stabilization, aquatic habitat restoration). May need **Pre-Construction Notification (PCN)**.
- **Individual Permit** — Larger or higher-impact fills; longer public interest review.
- **NWP 27** (aquatic habitat restoration) sometimes discussed for retention/ecological basins — **only if** the project truly fits NWP terms; PE/consultant must confirm.
- **Section 204** (beneficial use of dredged material) is a **separate** USACE civil-works authority sometimes used for **fill source** — not a substitute for 404 authorization of the discharge itself.

### Practical sequence with 404

1. Wetland delineation + **Jurisdictional Determination** (as needed).
2. Avoid/minimize fill in WOTUS; quantify impacts.
3. Select NWP vs individual; prepare PCN or full application.
4. IDEM 401 if required.
5. Do **not** assume IDNR CIF or FEMA CLOMR equals 404 approval.
